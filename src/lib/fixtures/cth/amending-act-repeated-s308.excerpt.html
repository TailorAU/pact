Part 1—Preliminary

1  Short title

  This Act is the Combatting Illicit Tobacco Act 2026.

2  Commencement

  Each provision of this Act specified in column 1 of the table commences in accordance with column 2 of the table.

3  Schedules

  Legislation that is specified in a Schedule to this Act is amended or repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned.

Part 2—Amendments of the Customs Act 1901

117C  Illicit tobacco: forfeiture of goods

  After subsection (2), insert a subsection dealing with forfeited illicit tobacco held by the Comptroller-General of Customs.

308  Amendment 1 of section 308 of the principal Act

  Subsection 308(1) is amended by omitting the words "tobacco products" and substituting "illicit tobacco".

308  Amendment 2 of section 308 of the principal Act

  After subsection 308(1), insert a subsection applying the penalty to a person who possesses illicit tobacco in commercial quantities.

308  Amendment 3 of section 308 of the principal Act

  Subsection 308(2) is amended by omitting "2 years" and substituting "5 years".

308  Amendment 4 of section 308 of the principal Act

  Subsection 308(3) is repealed and substituted with a provision on the evidential burden for the defendant.

308  Amendment 5 of section 308 of the principal Act

  At the end of section 308, add a note referring to the offence provisions in Part XIII.

228AA  Illicit tobacco: seizure without warrant

  An officer may seize illicit tobacco found in the course of exercising a power under this Part without a warrant.
